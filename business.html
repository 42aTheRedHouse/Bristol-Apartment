Why book direct for business

More space. Less cost. No complications.

Two hotel rooms in Bristol city centre run to £180–£250 per night. The Red House sleeps a team of up to five in a proper flat, at a fraction of that — with a kitchen, garden, and gigabit Wi-Fi included.

Space for a small team

Two bedrooms each with a double bed, a spacious lounge, and a garden. Up to 5 guests. More comfortable and more productive than separate hotel rooms.

Invoice available

Book direct and we provide a proper invoice for company expenses. Straightforward for your finance team.

Flexible arrival

Self check-in via lockbox with a PIN code sent ahead of arrival. No reception desk, no waiting around after a long journey.

Cut the food costs

A fully equipped kitchen means your team can cook in-house. Saves significantly on daily expenses, especially on longer stays.

Temple Meads on foot

Ten minutes' walk from Bristol Temple Meads. Direct trains to London Paddington, Cardiff, Birmingham, and beyond.

Gigabit Wi-Fi

Upgraded to a full gigabit connection. Handles video calls, remote desktop, large file transfers, and multiple users simultaneously without breaking a sweat.

Pre-arrival shopping

Order your essentials the day before you arrive and we'll stock the kitchen ready for your stay. Milk, coffee, breakfast basics — just let us know what you need.

Patio & BBQ

The private garden includes a patio with outdoor seating and a BBQ. A welcome way to wind down after a day's work — especially on a Bristol summer evening.
