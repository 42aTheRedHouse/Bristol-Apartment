The property

The Red House sleeps up to 5, with two double beds and a sofa for a fifth guest. It has a separate living room, a basic kitchen, a bathroom with shower, self check-in via lockbox, gigabit Wi‑Fi, and a small garden with a paved seating area and a good view.
